Relation before recital

Meaning enters before style

A typed map of the upstream Lattice engine: who supplies authority, how meaning becomes a bounded output plan, how candidates clear protected gates, and where hosts and ecosystem applications remain responsible.

Wrapper distinction: Text to Lattice infers a bounded contract from prose. It does not inherit the upstream engine's guarantee that a domain-authoritative caller supplied typed meaning.
